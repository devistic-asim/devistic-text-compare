# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Devistic Text Compare to be a respectful experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Disagree with the work, not the person.
- Accept correction, and offer it the same way.
- Focus on what helps people compare text privately and accurately.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, baiting, or sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, issues, or contributions that break this code, and may block further participation.

Report a problem through [github.com/devistic-asim](https://github.com/devistic-asim). There is no separate email address for this project.
